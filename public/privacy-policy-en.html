Privacy Policy

UNET Grade Calculator - Last updated: February 2026

This application does NOT collect, store, or share any personal user data.

1. Information We Collect

The UNET Grade Calculator application does not collect any personal information. All information you enter (grades, percentages, settings) is processed only on your device and is not sent to any external server.

2. Local Storage

The application uses your device's local storage only to save your preferences:

  • Theme preference (light/dark)
  • Language preference (Spanish/English)
  • Font size preference

This data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to third parties.

3. App Permissions

The application does not require special permissions to function. It does not access:

  • Your location
  • Your camera or microphone
  • Your contacts
  • Your personal files
  • Any other information on your device

4. Internet Connection

The application can work completely offline. No calls are made to external servers and no information of any kind is transmitted.

5. Third-Party Services

This application does not use analytics services, advertising, or any other third-party services that may collect information about you.

6. Data Security

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no risk of your information being compromised. All operations are performed locally on your device.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update this page with the new information. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
